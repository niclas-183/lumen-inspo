# Label additions (SaaS + Brand slice)

Used only where the frozen hero codebook had no honest fit. Color `other (…)` values follow the existing `other (name it)` rule and are not new codebook keys.

- `environmental-photo`: photographic landscape or interior as the visual anchor, without a human as the subject.
- `object-illustration`: large illustrated or 3D objects/landscape as the visual anchor (not a human).
